# Composer / Analyst Quick Reference

## Identity

`IDENTITY` = a musical entity or concept whose heredity is being modeled.

Examples registered for investigation: `TIGER`, `CODEC`, `DRAKKEN`, `STARSILK`, `SIEGE_WALL`, `BLOOD_ECLIPSE_RECURRENCE`, `MOTHER`.

Registration does not imply discovered genes.

## Gene

`GENE` = smallest evidence-supported musical behavior worth preserving across transformations.

Possible carriers:

`interval | rhythm | harmony | melody | timbre | vocal | structure | absence | production`

## Evidence

`AUDIO` — heard directly  
`STRUCTURAL` — notation/MIDI/transcription-derived  
`DOCUMENTARY` — prompt/note/metadata intent  
`CANONICAL` — explicit controlling canon

## Status

`UNKNOWN` → unsupported / not established  
`TENTATIVE` → evidence exists but insufficient recurrence  
`STRONGLY-SUPPORTED` → multiple independent families + realized evidence  
`CANONICAL` → explicit canonical evidence

## Transformation operators under investigation

`ALLIANCE(x)`  
`CORRUPTION(x)`  
`MEMORY(x)`  
`VIOLENCE(x)`  
`ABSENCE(x)`  
`HISTORICAL_RECURRENCE(x)`

Their exact musical mechanics must be learned from the corpus.

## Conformance classes

`CONFORMING` — deep grammar survives  
`BOUNDARY` — partial inheritance  
`COUNTERFEIT` — surface resemblance without registered genome  
`NONCONFORMING` — required gene missing or forbidden drift present  
`INSUFFICIENT-EVIDENCE` — registry cannot honestly judge

## Hard rules

1. Count independent source families, not files.
2. Do not turn genre convention into identity.
3. Do not turn prompt intent into audible evidence.
4. Do not force every identity into a melody.
5. Silence and deletion may be identity-bearing.
6. Contradictory corpus evidence beats elegant theory.
